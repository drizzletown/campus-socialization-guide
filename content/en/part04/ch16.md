# Chapter 16. Making Friends Without Forcing Closeness

## A situation you may recognize

You know many classmates but have no close friend, and feel pressured to attend every gathering.

## Understand the situation

Acquaintance, familiarity and trust are different relationship states. Friendship develops through mutual experience, reliability and consent; it does not follow automatically from contact lists or shared courses.

## When conditions differ

If you want more connection, begin with a manageable shared activity. If you value solitude and have adequate support, do not interpret quieter habits as a defect.

## Actions you can take

1. Start with a common interest or academic task.
2. notice reciprocal respect and boundaries.
3. suggest a low-pressure meeting.
4. allow relationships to deepen or remain casual..

## Boundaries and safety

No one owes another person constant availability. Being friendly does not authorize demands for private emotional support.
