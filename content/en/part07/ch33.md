# Chapter 33. Rumors, Rules and Reliable Information

## A situation you may recognize

A forwarded message claims that scholarship criteria or graduation rules changed, but no official notice is linked.

## Understand the situation

Social networks spread useful news and misinformation. Decisions about qualifications, costs and deadlines require authoritative, current sources that apply to your own program and cohort.

## When conditions differ

A classmate's accurate past experience may not apply this year. An official announcement may also need clarification about eligibility or implementation.

## Actions you can take

1. Find the original issuing authority.
2. check the effective date and target group.
3. record conflicting information.
4. ask the responsible office before making an irreversible choice..

## Boundaries and safety

Avoid spreading unverified allegations or students' private information. Uncertainty is better than inventing confidence.
