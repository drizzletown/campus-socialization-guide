# Chapter 11. Living with Roommates: Negotiate Rules, Respect Boundaries

## A situation you may recognize

One roommate plays videos late at night; another needs sleep. The first discussion turns into accusations in the group chat.

## Understand the situation

A shared room creates ongoing obligations, but it does not require identical habits. Separate institutional safety rules, collectively agreed arrangements, and individual preferences. Focus on observable effects and workable changes.

## When conditions differ

Different sleep schedules call for a practical agreement. Repeated harassment or threats require a different response, potentially involving residence staff or campus security.

## Actions you can take

1. Record the time and impact of the disturbance.
2. propose a specific quiet-hours arrangement.
3. agree on a review date.
4. use residential support channels if the issue persists..

## Boundaries and safety

Do not retaliate by recording or publishing a roommate's private life. If someone is in immediate danger, prioritize safety over negotiation.
