# Chapter 43. Money, Borrowing and Shared Responsibility

## A situation you may recognize

A close friend asks to borrow valuable equipment or money without defining return terms, then disputes what was agreed.

## Understand the situation

Trust and financial responsibility are related but distinct. Ownership, authorization, return dates and consequences should be clarified before an arrangement creates resentment or legal risk.

## When conditions differ

A small misunderstanding may be resolved through a written clarification. A serious dispute or repeated misuse may need formal consumer, legal or campus channels.

## Actions you can take

1. Check ownership and applicable rules.
2. confirm purpose and return terms in writing.
3. decline risks you cannot afford.
4. retain ordinary transaction records..

## Boundaries and safety

Do not lend passwords, restricted cards or institutional equipment contrary to policy. Friendship never requires accepting unlimited debt.
