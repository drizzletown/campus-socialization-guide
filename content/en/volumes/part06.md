# Part 6: Professional practice requires evidence and permission

Research, competitions and internships bring knowledge into situations with consequences for other people. A result must be checked; data and equipment require authorization; a task must fit the student's training and supervision. Professional confidence is not demonstrated by ignoring uncertainty or taking unapproved risks.

The five chapters follow entry into research, supervision, ethics, cross-disciplinary projects and internships. They show why a competent newcomer needs both opportunities to learn and clear responsibility boundaries. A group that makes requirements, review and safety procedures visible is better able to turn student effort into trustworthy work.
