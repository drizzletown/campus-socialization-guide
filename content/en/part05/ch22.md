# Chapter 22. Student Leadership: Match Responsibility with Authority

## A situation you may recognize

You become a club officer and suddenly handle money, approvals, personnel issues and tasks no one formally assigned to you.

## Understand the situation

Leadership is a defined organizational role, not a personal honor that makes you responsible for everything. Decisions, approvals, resources and oversight need clear owners.

## When conditions differ

Routine coordination may be delegated. Spending, discipline and access to protected information may require formal authorization and separation of duties.

## Actions you can take

1. Create a responsibility and approval table.
2. record decisions and deadlines.
3. publish clear financial processes.
4. provide members a way to challenge or decline extra work..

## Boundaries and safety

Do not spend money or disclose private records without authority. Resolve conflicts of interest openly and according to applicable rules.

> SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
