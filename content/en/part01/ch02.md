# Chapter 02. Map the People and Services That Can Support You

## A situation you may recognize

You need help with a course, a financial matter, and a difficult roommate situation, but are unsure whom to approach.

## Understand the situation

A network map is a guide to reachable support, not a competition to collect contacts. Friends, teachers, student services, and official procedures have different capacities and responsibilities.

## When conditions differ

A friend may listen without having authority to change a grade. A department office may explain an appeal procedure without needing a personal relationship with you.

## Actions you can take

1. Name the issue precisely.
2. find an official first point of contact.
3. identify another reliable source if that channel fails.
4. check who can actually decide or provide the needed service..

## Boundaries and safety

Do not assume that knowing someone grants access to their time, information, or institutional authority. Protect private contact details.

> SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
