# Chapter 27. Working with a Research Supervisor

## A situation you may recognize

A student receives vague tasks, infrequent feedback and conflicting expectations about progress.

## Understand the situation

Supervision is a working relationship involving academic authority, available time and professional responsibilities. The student should prepare evidence and questions; the supervisor should clarify standards and provide appropriate guidance.

## When conditions differ

If an instruction is ambiguous, request a concrete example or milestone. If conduct is abusive or safety is compromised, use an independent institutional channel.

## Actions you can take

1. Summarize the objective and your latest progress.
2. list decisions requiring input.
3. schedule a reasonable check-in.
4. record changes to scope and deliverables..

## Boundaries and safety

A supervisor's status does not authorize harassment, data misuse or unlimited personal work. Protect confidential concerns when seeking help.
