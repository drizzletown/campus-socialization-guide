# Chapter 49. Moving into a New City, Culture or Community

## A situation you may recognize

After moving, you discover that services, customs and academic expectations differ from what you know.

## Understand the situation

New environments change the relevant networks, rules and responsibilities. Some differences are formal requirements; others are conventions or individual preferences. Previous capabilities remain useful but may need adaptation.

## When conditions differ

When a procedure is unfamiliar, ask an authorized service instead of guessing. When treatment is discriminatory or unsafe, use local protection and support channels.

## Actions you can take

1. Find housing, health care, transport and emergency information.
2. learn the institution's official procedures.
3. build low-pressure connections.
4. maintain trusted relationships elsewhere..

## Boundaries and safety

Do not generalize a community from one encounter. Follow local laws and respect others' privacy while protecting your own rights.
