# Chapter 47. Balancing Roles Without Pleasing Everyone

## A situation you may recognize

Coursework, family responsibilities, a club office and a team deadline all demand attention at once.

## Understand the situation

Different relationships create different kinds of obligations. Separate urgent safety duties, formal requirements, agreed deliverables, optional activities and emotional expectations. No schedule can satisfy unlimited claims.

## When conditions differ

A temporary conflict may need a revised deadline. Repeated new tasks without support or authority may need redistribution or withdrawal.

## Actions you can take

1. List commitments and deadlines.
2. identify who depends on each task.
3. negotiate realistic changes early.
4. document the handover and review workload afterward..

## Boundaries and safety

Do not disappear from critical duties, but do not accept endless additions because someone calls you dependable.
