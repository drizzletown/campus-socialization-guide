# Chapter 38. Job Hunting: Match Evidence to Real Work

## A situation you may recognize

You send applications without responses and begin to believe you have no worthwhile skills.

## Understand the situation

Recruitment outcomes depend on opportunity supply, timing, qualification filters and how evidence matches role requirements. A rejection does not identify its cause by itself.

## When conditions differ

A resume with unclear project evidence needs revision. A fee-demanding recruiter or request for bank codes needs independent fraud checks rather than better self-promotion.

## Actions you can take

1. Read the job's actual duties.
2. map them to verifiable projects.
3. describe your role and outcomes honestly.
4. check employer legitimacy and contract conditions..

## Boundaries and safety

Do not submit confidential employer materials or invented experience. Never pay an unverified recruiter merely for the promise of a job.

> SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
