SITUATION 07

From Following a Lecture to Learning Independently

You understand examples in class but cannot solve a modified question alone and hesitate to attend office hours.

Compare causes and options

If you cannot explain the underlying concept, revisit fundamentals. If you understand it but misread the assignment, clarify requirements rather than endlessly practicing unrelated problems.

  1. Attempt one unfamiliar task unaided.
  2. identify the exact sticking point.
  3. bring your attempt to a published help session.
  4. test your understanding on a new example afterwards..

Make Group Assignments Reliable and Fair

Only two members finish a group project while others neither reply nor deliver, and the deadline is close.

SITUATION 11

Living with Roommates: Negotiate Rules, Respect Boundaries

One roommate plays videos late at night; another needs sleep. The first discussion turns into accusations in the group chat.

Compare causes and options

Different sleep schedules call for a practical agreement. Repeated harassment or threats require a different response, potentially involving residence staff or campus security.

  1. Record the time and impact of the disturbance.
  2. propose a specific quiet-hours arrangement.
  3. agree on a review date.
  4. use residential support channels if the issue persists..

SITUATION 13

Money, Loans, Part-Time Work and Financial Aid

Friends pressure you to split a costly purchase or lend money, while a part-time job advertises unusually high pay after an up-front fee.

Compare causes and options

For a routine shared purchase, negotiate a budget and clear payment terms. For fees before employment or requests for identity documents, stop and independently verify legitimacy.

  1. Check the total cost and who is liable.
  2. verify aid or employment through official channels.
  3. keep essential receipts.
  4. decline unaffordable or unsafe commitments..

SITUATION 19

Intimate Relationships: Consent, Care and Independence

A partner expects instant replies and constant location sharing, describing hesitation as proof of insufficient love.

Compare causes and options

A difference in communication preferences invites negotiation. Monitoring, coercion, threats or stalking are safety concerns rather than routine romantic disagreements.

  1. State your needs and availability.
  2. ask what both people freely agree to.
  3. set revisitable limits on time and information sharing.
  4. seek support if pressure escalates..

Research Ethics, Laboratory Safety and Authorship

A deadline approaches, and team members propose skipping an approval, hiding anomalous data or deciding author order informally.

Compare causes and options

An ordinary contribution disagreement requires documented discussion under discipline and journal rules. Falsification, privacy breaches or unsafe experiments need separate reporting and protection.

  2. maintain data provenance and version history.
  3. discuss contributions early.
  4. report deviations and incidents through authorized channels..

Compare causes and options

  3. frame a specific question for review.
