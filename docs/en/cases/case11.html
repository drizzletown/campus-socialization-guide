WORKED CASE 11 / 12

False Stories from Anonymous Accounts

What happened

An anonymous account spreads a rumor about a student, and friends recommend public retaliation.

Understand the different causes

A rumor may entail misinformation, privacy harm or a credible threat. A public argument can amplify the incident.

What can be done

Save essential evidence, assess safety, use platform reporting and appropriate campus channels, and clarify only verified facts when needed.

Safety and boundaries

Blackmail, stalking or immediate threats should be treated as protection matters.
