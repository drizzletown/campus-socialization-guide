WORKED CASE 01 / 12

Considering a Research Position

What happened

Two capable students have not applied to a lab. One is already committed to a competition; the other does not know the project's requirements.

Understand the different causes

Identical behavior can reflect different conditions. The first student may need to compare time commitments; the second needs a clear entry route and task description.

What can be done

Ask for the expected tasks, supervision, time and prerequisites. Decide whether to apply, try a small activity or defer without treating the outcome as a character judgment.

Safety and boundaries

Research access and training require legitimate supervision. Do not commit to work you cannot safely or responsibly perform.
