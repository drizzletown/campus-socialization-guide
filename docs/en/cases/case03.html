WORKED CASE 03 / 12

Late-Night Videos in a Shared Room

What happened

One roommate needs to sleep; another frequently watches videos aloud after lights-out, and the discussion becomes accusatory.

Understand the different causes

Shared space requires attention to actual impact, formal housing policies and feasible agreements rather than judgments about personality.

What can be done

Describe the noise, propose headphones or a quiet period, review the agreement, and use residence services if necessary.

Safety and boundaries

Threats or repeated serious harassment are safety issues, not ordinary bargaining problems.
