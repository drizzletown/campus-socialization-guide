WORKED CASE 04 / 12

Requesting a Grade Review

What happened

A student suspects an exam question was incorrectly marked and wonders whether a friend should ask the instructor for a favor.

Understand the different causes

An evaluation dispute requires the current criteria, evidence and the formal review channel. Personal contacts do not grant marking authority.

What can be done

Check the rubric and deadline, identify the disputed point, submit a factual request, and keep the reply.

Safety and boundaries

Do not seek preferential treatment or expose another student's marks.
