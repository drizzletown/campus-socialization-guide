Graduation with an Uncertain Direction

What happened

A graduating student has good marks but no fixed career plan and feels pressured to commit to a ten-year strategy.

Understand the different causes

Graduation alters roles and access to campus services. Uncertainty can be managed with realistic short-term goals and revisable choices.

What can be done

List transferable work, compare two viable next steps, identify practical support and set a three-month review point.

Safety and boundaries

Prioritize safe housing, income and essential care over an abstract image of success.
