WORKED CASE 09 / 12

Customer Data on a Personal Laptop

What happened

An intern is told to copy customer names and contact details onto a personal computer to work from home.

Understand the different causes

A legitimate business task may still involve an unauthorized data transfer. Access and security rules apply to interns too.

What can be done

Check data classification, request a compliant workspace or authorized sample and obtain proper approval before transferring anything.

Safety and boundaries

Do not send confidential data to personal email or cloud storage.
