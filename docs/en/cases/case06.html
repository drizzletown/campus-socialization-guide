WORKED CASE 06 / 12

Club Membership Fees Without Accounting

What happened

A club collects fees but refuses to explain spending; members who ask are accused of disloyalty.

Understand the different causes

Budget questions concern shared resources and authorized procedures, not personal hostility. Keep allegations separate from verified evidence.

What can be done

Review club rules, request financial records, seek oversight from the designated campus office and decide whether to remain.

Safety and boundaries

Never publish unsupported theft accusations or send further payments to unverified accounts.
