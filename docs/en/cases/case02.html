WORKED CASE 02 / 12

A Team Project Falls on Two People

What happened

A five-person programming team is near its deadline, but only two members have submitted usable work.

Understand the different causes

Incomplete work may reflect unclear responsibility, unavailable tools, missing skills, illness or avoidance. Identify which condition is present before assigning motives.

What can be done

Make deliverables and permissions explicit; ask about blockers; document progress; request the instructor's help if inequity persists.

Safety and boundaries

Do not falsify contribution records or publicly shame classmates.
