From Campus to Work: Choice, Applications and Risk

Academic qualifications matter, but entering employment also requires access to reliable information, evidence of work, an understanding of professional responsibilities and the ability to decide whether an opportunity is acceptable. Different problems in a job search call for different responses.

Route A: I receive no response to applications

Look at role requirements, eligibility and timing before concluding that your skills are inadequate. Compare each application with the role's actual tasks. Describe one or two projects using the problem, your responsibility, the method used and verifiable outcomes. Ask a career service to review the match, not simply to make the résumé longer.

Route B: I am unsure what direction to take

Compare realistic alternatives: further study, paid work, internships, professional training or a temporary exploration project. Consider financial constraints, preparation time, typical work and reversibility. Talk with informed people from more than one background, while preserving your own responsibility to choose.

Route C: An offer seems suspicious or unsafe

Verify the employer through independent official sources. Upfront recruitment fees, requests for bank verification codes, pressure to send identity documents through personal chat accounts, or guaranteed employment without a credible process are serious warning signs. Stop, verify and seek legitimate support. If you have already disclosed sensitive information or paid money, contact appropriate banks, platforms or authorities promptly.

Internship obligations

Check supervision, approved tools, data permissions, work hours and the evaluation process. Do not copy workplace data to personal storage to appear helpful. Record achievements without revealing trade secrets or other protected information.

A practical decision rule

Every opportunity should be examined for demonstrated skill fit, legitimate access, clear responsibility, real contribution and room for informed choice. Neither a prestigious label nor a friend's referral replaces these checks.
