Part 7: Technology speeds up connections without removing responsibility

Messaging systems, social media and AI make information more accessible and actions faster. They can also create overload, social comparison, plausible misinformation or unauthorized uses of private data. Speed does not make an announcement official; fluent output does not make a claim true.

This part connects online coordination, public identity, source evaluation, AI and digital safety. It develops a transferable method: check where information comes from, who has permission to use it, what can go wrong and who is responsible for correction. Digital systems should support judgment and access, not require continuous availability or endless self-display.
