Part 10: Development continues through changing environments

Graduation ends a university course of study but not the need to understand people, institutions and choices. New work roles, cities and communities change which resources and relationships are available. Failure may require a new explanation; competing commitments may require an honest adjustment; new authority may create responsibilities toward others.

The final chapters invite students to treat growth as continuing reconstruction rather than a competition for permanent maturity. A lasting capability is being able to investigate unfamiliar conditions, participate responsibly, revise commitments and help make shared institutions more understandable and fair to the next person who arrives.
