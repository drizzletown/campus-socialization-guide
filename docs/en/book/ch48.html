Chapter 48. Develop an Independent Position with Reasons

A situation you may recognize

Teachers, family and friends recommend different futures, and you worry that following anyone's advice is a loss of independence.

Understand the situation

Autonomy means understanding goals and evidence, accepting appropriate responsibility, and being willing to revise judgments. Agreement with others can be entirely autonomous when chosen thoughtfully.

When conditions differ

If facts remain uncertain, seek another informed perspective. If a decision is hard to reverse, allow more verification time instead of treating pressure as a deadline.

Actions you can take

  1. Write what matters to you.
  2. compare evidence and consequences.
  3. identify who can answer missing questions.
  4. choose a route and a time to review it..

Boundaries and safety

Do not confuse rebellion with autonomy or loyalty with consent. Your principles can evolve when conditions change.

SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
