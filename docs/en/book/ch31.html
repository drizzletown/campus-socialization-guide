Chapter 31. Group Chats and Instant Messaging Without Constant Overload

A situation you may recognize

Important instructions are buried beneath hundreds of chat messages, and members are blamed for missing a deadline.

Understand the situation

Digital communication creates fast connections, but reliable coordination still needs source authority, accessible records and realistic response expectations. A group chat is not a substitute for official notice.

When conditions differ

If a message is an informal suggestion, verify before acting. If it is an official requirement, confirm it against the designated source and record the deadline.

Actions you can take

  1. Separate official announcements from conversation.
  2. maintain a private task list.
  3. ask who is accountable for changes.
  4. mute nonurgent messages without ignoring agreed responsibilities..

Boundaries and safety

Never treat a forwarded payment request or personal-data request as legitimate solely because many people shared it.

SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
