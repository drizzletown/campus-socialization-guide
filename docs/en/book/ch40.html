Chapter 40. Starting a Project or Venture with Real Responsibilities

A situation you may recognize

Friends propose a startup before agreeing on ownership, funds, data access or the work each person will do.

Understand the situation

Creative energy is valuable, but practical projects need evidence of user need, valid permissions, roles, financial responsibility and exit arrangements. Friendship cannot replace an agreement.

When conditions differ

A low-cost prototype can test interest without large risk. Paid services, personal data and shared intellectual property call for explicit rules and professional advice where appropriate.

Actions you can take

  1. Interview prospective users respectfully.
  2. test a small solution.
  3. record contributions and spending.
  4. decide whether to continue only after reviewing evidence..

Boundaries and safety

Do not collect private data without a lawful basis. Be clear about who bears debts and how user information is handled if the project closes.

SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
