Chapter 34. Use AI as an Assistant, Not as Your Final Judge

A situation you may recognize

You ask an AI system whether to report a conflict or accept an internship and receive a confident recommendation based on incomplete details.

Understand the situation

AI can organize questions, compare options and improve clarity, but it does not know all relevant people, rules or actual resources. Its answers may be wrong or unsupported.

When conditions differ

For low-stakes drafting, AI may help you prepare a respectful message. For health, contracts, discipline or safety, confirm information with appropriate professionals and official sources.

Actions you can take

  1. State the goal without exposing sensitive facts.
  2. ask the system to list assumptions.
  3. independently verify important claims.
  4. make and own the final decision..

Boundaries and safety

Do not disclose someone else's secrets to a chatbot. Avoid labeling people with psychological diagnoses based on AI text.

SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
