Chapter 04. Five Ways Social Relationships Change

A situation you may recognize

Joining a club creates new acquaintances, duties, shared projects and unexpected pressure on your study time.

Understand the situation

The five change mechanisms are connection formation, learning rules, exchange of value, evolution of roles, and generation of future networks. They can overlap without occurring in a fixed order.

When conditions differ

More contacts can create opportunity or overload. A role can deepen your skills while also producing unreasonable dependencies.

Actions you can take

  1. Identify what changed in the relationship.
  2. ask which rule or role now matters.
  3. check reciprocal responsibilities and available support.
  4. keep, renegotiate or leave the arrangement based on evidence..

Boundaries and safety

Do not assume that larger networks, busier schedules or higher titles mean improvement. Ending a harmful commitment can be constructive.

SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
