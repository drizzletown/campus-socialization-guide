Chapter 08. Communicate with Instructors Professionally

A situation you may recognize

You want feedback on a grade but fear that questioning the mark will appear disrespectful.

Understand the situation

Teaching relationships involve both shared learning objectives and differences in evaluation authority. Effective contact is specific, evidence-based, and made through the appropriate channel.

When conditions differ

For conceptual confusion, request an explanation or office-hours help. For a possible grading error, follow the published review procedure with the relevant work and marking criteria.

Actions you can take

  1. Read the syllabus.
  2. prepare the specific question and evidence.
  3. contact the appropriate instructor or office politely.
  4. record the clarification and next action..

Boundaries and safety

Do not seek special marks through private connections. Distinguish critical feedback on work from personal humiliation or threats.

SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
