Chapter 45. An Immediate Crisis: Protect Life Before Reflecting on Growth

A situation you may recognize

An accident, serious threat or medical emergency occurs during a campus event.

Understand the situation

In an emergency the priority is reducing harm and reaching people with the right training and authority. Students should not be required to provide treatment, rescue or investigation beyond their competence.

When conditions differ

A routine disagreement can wait for a conversation. Serious injury, imminent danger or a potentially life-threatening situation requires urgent professional intervention.

Actions you can take

  1. Make sure you are not entering danger.
  2. contact the locally appropriate emergency or campus response service.
  3. give the location and observed facts.
  4. follow qualified instructions..

Boundaries and safety

Do not move a seriously injured person unless the scene itself is unsafe or responders direct you. Avoid spreading images or identifying information.

SSCM reading note: Consider what you can demonstrate (C), what support is reachable (N), who has the relevant responsibility (R), what useful contribution can actually be made (V), and whether you have an informed, revisable choice (A). These are questions for this situation, not a score.
